# Gate 06 Report: Multimodal Feature Fusion

## Objective
Combine Stage-1 electrical fingerprint features with EMAT acoustic features into a unified multimodal representation, supporting the three run modes required by Section 5.5 of `01_project_details.md` (Electrical-only, Acoustic-only, and Primary Fused mode), and document the fused feature schema with real-unit demonstration across 20 units.

## What Was Built
1. **Multimodal Fusion Engine (`/backend/fusion/feature_fusion.py`)**:
   - `MultimodalFusionEngine`: Orchestrates extraction of 6 Stage-1 electrical features and couples with 7 EMAT acoustic features.
   - `RunMode` support:
     - `electrical_only`: 6-dimensional feature vector.
     - `acoustic_only`: 7-dimensional feature vector.
     - `fused`: 13-dimensional joint feature vector (primary mode).
   - Dynamic coupling: battery units from the lot simulator map to their underlying physical degradation state (Healthy, Mild, Moderate, Plating-associated, Severe), driving both electrical pulse characteristics and EMAT acoustic propagation consistently.
2. **Test Suite (`/tests/test_feature_fusion.py`)**:
   - Validates on-demand generation of all 3 modes from the same unit.
   - Confirms exact vector concatenation consistency.
   - Audits fused feature schema documentation.
   - Executes 20-unit demonstration on real units.

## Fused Feature Schema

| Index | Feature Field | Modality Source | Standard Units | Physical Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `ocv_v` | Electrical (Stage 1) | V | Open-circuit voltage measured at rest |
| 1 | `dcir_mohm` | Electrical (Stage 1) | mΩ | DC internal-resistance proxy ($\Delta V / \Delta I$ from pulse) |
| 2 | `relaxation_slope` | Electrical (Stage 1) | V/s | Voltage-relaxation slope immediately following pulse |
| 3 | `relaxation_curvature` | Electrical (Stage 1) | V/s² | Voltage-relaxation curvature following pulse |
| 4 | `temperature_response_c` | Electrical (Stage 1) | °C | Surface temperature rise during current pulse |
| 5 | `cell_imbalance_mv` | Electrical (Stage 1) | mV | Module/cell voltage imbalance or spread |
| 6 | `tof_us` | Acoustic (EMAT DSP) | μs | Ultrasonic Time-of-Flight arrival delay across cell layers |
| 7 | `peak_amplitude` | Acoustic (EMAT DSP) | V | Matched-filter envelope correlation peak amplitude |
| 8 | `integrated_energy` | Acoustic (EMAT DSP) | V²·s | Integrated energy in echo correlation window |
| 9 | `echo_width_us` | Acoustic (EMAT DSP) | μs | Echo pulse compression FWHM width |
| 10 | `attenuation_db` | Acoustic (EMAT DSP) | dB | Acoustic signal attenuation relative to healthy reference |
| 11 | `snr_db` | Acoustic (EMAT DSP) | dB | Post-processing Signal-to-Noise Ratio after matched filter |
| 12 | `repeatability` | Acoustic (EMAT DSP) | ratio | Cross-correlation repeatability across repeated pulse bursts |
